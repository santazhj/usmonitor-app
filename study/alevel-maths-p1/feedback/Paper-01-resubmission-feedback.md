## Q12(b) 点评

学生写的是 "The horizontal direction is compressed to 0.5 times its original size, while the vertical direction is stretched to 3 times its original size." 意思完全正确，两个方向和两个系数都有，给满分。

考试标准措辞请改用：

- **Stretch parallel to the $x$-axis, scale factor $\tfrac12$**
- **Stretch parallel to the $y$-axis, scale factor 3**

评分标准认的关键词是 *stretch*、*parallel to the … axis*、*scale factor*。"compressed" 在 Cambridge 评分里通常也接受，但 "stretch, scale factor ½" 永远不会被扣分。
