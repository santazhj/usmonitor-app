**1** &nbsp; $2(x-3)^2-11$: B1 for $a=2$, B1 for $b=-3$ (and $c=-11$); B1 min value $-11$ at $x=3$. **[3]**

**2** &nbsp; Use $\sin^2\theta=1-\cos^2\theta$: $2-2\cos^2\theta+3\cos\theta=3$ (M1) $\Rightarrow 2\cos^2\theta-3\cos\theta+1=0 \Rightarrow (2\cos\theta-1)(\cos\theta-1)=0$ (M1). $\cos\theta=\tfrac12$: $\theta=60^\circ,\,300^\circ$ (A1); $\cos\theta=1$: $\theta=0^\circ,\,360^\circ$ (A1). Four answers: $0^\circ, 60^\circ, 300^\circ, 360^\circ$. **[4]**

**3** &nbsp; Midpoint $(4,1)$ (B1). Gradient $AB=\frac{-3-5}{7-1}=-\frac43$, so perpendicular gradient $=\frac34$ (M1 A1). Line: $y-1=\frac34(x-4)\Rightarrow 3x-4y-8=0$ (A1). **[4]**

**4(a)** &nbsp; $ar=12$, $\frac{a}{1-r}=64$ (B1 both). Substitute $a=64(1-r)$: $64r(1-r)=12\Rightarrow16r^2-16r+3=0$ (M1, AG). Factorise $(4r-1)(4r-3)=0$: $r=\tfrac14$ or $r=\tfrac34$ (A1). **[3]**
**4(b)** &nbsp; $r=\tfrac34$, $a=16$ (B1 FT). $S_5=\dfrac{16(1-(3/4)^5)}{1-3/4}=\dfrac{781}{16}=48.8$ (3 s.f.) (M1 A1 — accept 48.8 or 48.8125). **[2]**

**5(a)** &nbsp; $\mathrm{fg}(x)=2(x^2-4x)+3=2x^2-8x+3$ (B1). $2x^2-8x+3=13\Rightarrow x^2-4x-5=0\Rightarrow(x-5)(x+1)=0$ (M1). $x=5$ only (reject $x=-1$ since domain of g is $x\ge2$) (A1). **[3]**
**5(b)** &nbsp; $y=(x-2)^2-4$ (M1 complete the square or use formula), $x=2\pm\sqrt{y+4}$; take $+$ because $x\ge2$: $\mathrm{g}^{-1}(x)=2+\sqrt{x+4}$ (A1). Domain $x\ge-4$ (B1, = range of g). **[3]**

**6(a)** &nbsp; Arc $AB=8\times1.2=9.6$ (B1). $OC=8\cos1.2=2.899$, $AC=8\sin1.2=7.456$ (M1). Perimeter $=9.6+(8-2.899)+7.456=22.2$ cm (A1; 22.16). **[3]**
**6(b)** &nbsp; Sector area $=\tfrac12\times8^2\times1.2=38.4$ (B1). Triangle $OAC=\tfrac12\times2.899\times7.456=10.81$ (M1). Region $=38.4-10.81=27.6$ cm$^2$ (A1; 27.59). **[3]**

**7(a)** &nbsp; $\frac{dy}{dx}=3x^2-12x+9$ (B1). $=3(x-1)(x-3)=0\Rightarrow x=1,\,3$ (M1). Points $(1,5)$ and $(3,1)$ (A1). $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}=6x-12$: at $x=1$ negative $\Rightarrow$ maximum at $(1,5)$; at $x=3$ positive $\Rightarrow$ minimum at $(3,1)$ (A1). **[4]**
**7(b)** &nbsp; At $x=2$: $y=3$, $\frac{dy}{dx}=12-24+9=-3$ (B1). Normal gradient $=\tfrac13$ (M1). $y-3=\tfrac13(x-2)\Rightarrow x-3y+7=0$ (A1). **[3]**

**8(a)** &nbsp; $4x-x^2=x\Rightarrow x(3-x)=0$ (M1); $P=(3,3)$ (A1). **[2]**
**8(b)** &nbsp; Area $=\int_0^3\big[(4x-x^2)-x\big]dx=\int_0^3(3x-x^2)\,dx$ (M1 subtract, M1 integrate) $=\left[\tfrac32x^2-\tfrac13x^3\right]_0^3=\tfrac{27}{2}-9$ (A1) $=\tfrac92=4.5$ (A1). **[4]**
**8(c)** &nbsp; $V=\pi\int_0^3(4x-x^2)^2dx=\pi\int_0^3(16x^2-8x^3+x^4)\,dx$ (M1 expand & set up) $=\pi\left[\tfrac{16}{3}x^3-2x^4+\tfrac15x^5\right]_0^3$ (A1) $=\pi(144-162+48.6)=30.6\pi$ (A1; $=\tfrac{153}{5}\pi$). **[3]**

**9(a)** &nbsp; LHS $=\dfrac{(1+\sin\theta)-(1-\sin\theta)}{(1-\sin\theta)(1+\sin\theta)}$ (M1) $=\dfrac{2\sin\theta}{1-\sin^2\theta}=\dfrac{2\sin\theta}{\cos^2\theta}$ (M1, uses $\sin^2+\cos^2=1$) $=\dfrac{2\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}\cdot\dfrac{1}{\cos\theta}=\dfrac{2\tan\theta}{\cos\theta}$ (A1, AG). **[3]**
**9(b)** &nbsp; $\dfrac{2\tan\theta}{\cos\theta}=8\tan\theta\Rightarrow\tan\theta\left(\dfrac{2}{\cos\theta}-8\right)=0$ (M1 — do **not** divide through by $\tan\theta$ without considering $\tan\theta=0$). $\tan\theta=0\Rightarrow\theta=180^\circ$ (A1; $0^\circ$ and $360^\circ$ are excluded by the strict inequality). $\cos\theta=\tfrac14\Rightarrow\theta=75.5^\circ$ (A1), $284.5^\circ$ (A1). **[4]**
**9(c)** &nbsp; $2x-\tfrac{\pi}{3}$ lies in $\left[-\tfrac{\pi}{3},\tfrac{5\pi}{3}\right]$; $\sin=\tfrac12$ at $\tfrac{\pi}{6}$ and $\tfrac{5\pi}{6}$ (M1). $2x=\tfrac{\pi}{2}\Rightarrow x=\tfrac{\pi}{4}$ (A1); $2x=\tfrac{7\pi}{6}\Rightarrow x=\tfrac{7\pi}{12}$ (A1). **[3]**
